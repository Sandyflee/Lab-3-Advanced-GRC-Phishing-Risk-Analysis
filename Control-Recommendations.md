## Bonus: Break-Even Analysis

Total security investment:

**$490,000**

Cost of one major breach:

**$4,350,000**

Break-even:

$490,000 / $4,350,000 = **0.1126 breaches**

Therefore, approximately **0.113 avoided breaches** mathematically covers the investment.

In practical terms, preventing **one major breach** would more than justify the $490,000 investment.

Net benefit from preventing one breach:

$4,350,000 - $490,000 = **$3,860,000**

## Final Recommendation

GlobalTech Inc. should prioritize MFA first, followed by security awareness training and email security. Continuous monitoring and behavior analytics should provide additional layers of defense and measurement.
