# Phase 4: Executive Briefing

**To:** Board of Directors, CISO, CFO  
**From:** GRC Risk Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Subject:** CRITICAL: Quantitative Phishing Risk Assessment and Financial Exposure

## 1. Executive Summary

The analysis of three phishing simulations identified significant human-related cybersecurity risk across GlobalTech Inc.

Campaign B, the IT System Password Reset simulation, produced the highest credential submission rate at **30%**.

If this rate were representative of the organization's 2,000 employees, approximately **600 employees could potentially submit credentials** during a similar attack.

Using the assumed 25% probability of a major breach following credential compromise, this represents an expected **150 breach events** and an estimated financial exposure of **$652.5 million**.

Immediate implementation of **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is recommended as the highest-priority control.

## Conclusion

The phishing simulations demonstrate significant human-related cybersecurity risk. Campaign B represents the most urgent risk because of its 30% credential submission rate.

The analysis supports immediate MFA deployment combined with targeted security awareness training, advanced email protection, continuous monitoring, and behavioral analytics.

A $490,000 security investment is strongly justified under the lab's assumptions and could reduce modeled financial exposure by approximately $587.5 million.
