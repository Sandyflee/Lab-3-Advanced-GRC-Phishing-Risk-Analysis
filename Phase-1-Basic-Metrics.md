# Phase 1: Basic Metric Calculations

## Step 1: Fundamental Rates

**Formula:**  
Rate = (Number of Events / Number of Emails Sent) × 100

| Campaign | Email Open Rate | Click-Through Rate | Credential Submission Rate | Data Entry Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 82% | 47% | 18% | 5% |
| B | 80% | 60% | 30% | 10% |
| C | 90% | 75% | 15% | 10% |

### Campaign A
- Open Rate = (82/100) × 100 = **82%**
- Click Rate = (47/100) × 100 = **47%**
- Credential Rate = (18/100) × 100 = **18%**
- Data Entry Rate = (5/100) × 100 = **5%**

### Campaign B
- Open Rate = (200/250) × 100 = **80%**
- Click Rate = (150/250) × 100 = **60%**
- Credential Rate = (75/250) × 100 = **30%**
- Data Entry Rate = (25/250) × 100 = **10%**

### Campaign C
- Open Rate = (72/80) × 100 = **90%**
- Click Rate = (60/80) × 100 = **75%**
- Credential Rate = (12/80) × 100 = **15%**
- Data Entry Rate = (8/80) × 100 = **10%**

## Step 2: Efficiency Metrics

### Open-to-Click Conversion
Formula: Clicks / Opens × 100

- Campaign A = 47/82 × 100 = **57.3%**
- Campaign B = 150/200 × 100 = **75.0%**
- Campaign C = 60/72 × 100 = **83.3%**

### Post-Click Credential Submission
Formula: Credentials Submitted / Clicks × 100

- Campaign A = 18/47 × 100 = **38.3%**
- Campaign B = 75/150 × 100 = **50.0%**
- Campaign C = 12/60 × 100 = **20.0%**

## Key Observation
Campaign B is the most dangerous credential-phishing scenario because it has the highest credential submission rate (30%) and the highest post-click credential submission rate (50%).
