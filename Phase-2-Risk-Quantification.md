# Phase 2: Advanced Risk Quantification

## Step 3: Organizational Risk Exposure

Campaign B credential rate:

75 / 250 = **30%**

Projected credential exposure:

2,000 × 0.30 = **600 employees**

Expected breaches:

600 × 25% = **150 expected breach events**

## Step 4: Financial Impact Analysis

### Potential Financial Loss
Single breach cost = **$4,350,000**

Expected breaches = **150**

Total exposure:

$4,350,000 × 150 = **$652,500,000**

### Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

Formula:

ALE = SLE × ARO

- SLE = $4,350,000
- ARO = 150

ALE = $4,350,000 × 150 = **$652,500,000**

## Step 5: Control Effectiveness

### MFA Cost-Benefit Analysis

MFA cost:

$45 × 2,000 = **$90,000**

Risk reduction:

$652,500,000 × 0.999 = **$651,847,500**

Net benefit:

$651,847,500 - $90,000 = **$651,757,500**

ROI:

[($651,847,500 - $90,000) / $90,000] × 100 = **724,175%**

### Security Awareness Training

Training cost:

$50 × 2,000 = **$100,000**

New click-through rate:

30% × (1 - 0.60) = **12%**

New projected credentials stolen:

2,000 × 0.12 = **240**

New expected breaches:

240 × 25% = **60**

New ALE:

60 × $4,350,000 = **$261,000,000**

Risk reduction:

$652,500,000 - $261,000,000 = **$391,500,000**

Net benefit:

$391,500,000 - $100,000 = **$391,400,000**

ROI:

($391,400,000 / $100,000) × 100 = **391,400%**
